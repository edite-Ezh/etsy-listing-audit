# Etsy Listing Audit Methodology

## Purpose

Determine whether the listing consistently communicates one purchasable product to one primary buyer intent, then identify the highest-impact conversion and positioning improvements.

## Signal hierarchy

Evaluate evidence in this order:

1. Hero image: the promise buyers understand before reading.
2. Title and visible price: product identity and click expectation.
3. Variations, quantity and personalization: what can actually be ordered.
4. Gallery: proof, dimensions, materials, use and risk reduction.
5. Description and policies: supporting detail.
6. Category and tags: discovery signals.
7. Reviews, favorites, carts, listing age and EverBee estimates: demand and friction evidence.

Do not let a good description repair a misleading hero. A buyer should not need to open the description to learn whether the price is for one item or a pair.

## Buyer intent

State:

- **What is actually sold:** physical product, quantity, material, construction, customization and included components.
- **Primary buyer intent:** the single most strongly supported problem, use or occasion.
- **Secondary buyer intents:** no more than three, and only when the listing provides real evidence.

Terms such as `Christmas gift`, `bed socks`, `therapeutic socks`, `wedding gift`, or `nursery decor` do not justify a separate listing by themselves. They become valid hero directions only when supported by a distinct photo, product configuration, benefit, audience and purchasing context.

## Hero analysis

Analyze the hero separately from the gallery. Ask:

- What product, quantity, color, scale and use does the hero imply?
- Does the purchasable default match it?
- Can the buyer understand the finished result without reading?
- Is the hero proving the primary intent or only showing a detail?
- Does it show a pair while the offer contains one item?
- Does it show a trim, color, accessory or construction that is not selectable?

Then analyze the gallery as a sequence. Each image should have a purchasing function: context, scale, dimensions, material proof, construction, privacy, color choice, care, packaging, customization or comparison.

## Signal alignment score

Give a 1–10 score:

- 9–10: one clear product and intent; hero, offer and proof agree.
- 7–8: strong identity with a few correctable decision gaps.
- 5–6: attractive listing with meaningful quantity, variant, category or claim conflicts.
- 3–4: multiple competing identities or unsupported promises.
- 1–2: the buyer cannot reliably determine what will arrive.

Explain the score through observable signals, not taste.

## Demand interpretation

Treat Etsy live data as current. Treat EverBee as estimated and possibly delayed.

The Etsy footer `Listed on` date can reflect renewal or relisting. Do not call the product new when EverBee age, reviews or sales show a longer history.

Interpret combinations:

- high favorites + low conversion: strong visual interest with buying friction;
- strong reviews + low conversion: product quality may be good while the offer is unclear;
- new listing + favorites but no sales: early interest, not proven demand;
- sustained sales + narrow intent: a candidate for carefully separated hero variants;
- many sizes and made-to-order production: operationally supports separate hero listings, but each listing still needs a distinct intent.

## Price and delivery

Use the smallest displayed variant as the entry-price basis and identify the largest displayed price. Never imply the entry price applies to every size.

For each destination calculate:

`total = product price + shipping price`

`shipping ratio = shipping price / product price × 100`

Convert Etsy ETA dates into minimum and maximum calendar days from the audit date. Record visible VAT, tax, customs or import warnings without predicting charges Etsy does not show.

Interpret the ratio in context. A fixed shipping price can be a major conversion barrier for a small variant and negligible for a large one.

## Missing visual proof

Recommend only images that add a missing function. Common high-value needs:

- complete product in its intended setting;
- `1 item` versus `pair/set` diagram;
- dimension chart in centimetres and inches;
- how to measure and fullness/fit calculation;
- material composition and close-up;
- day/night or before/after benefit comparison;
- actual photos of each selectable construction;
- personalization example;
- color identity of the hero variant;
- packaging or gift wrapping when gift intent is used;
- short product video.

Do not recommend duplicates of information already clearly shown.

## Hero separation

Provide two lists:

1. **Hero ideas that can be separated now:** already supported by product capability and visual evidence.
2. **Hero ideas to test for demand:** plausible but not yet sufficiently proven.

A legitimate separate hero should change at least two important signals, such as audience, room/use, color/design, construction, quantity, benefit, season or price package. Do not create duplicates that differ only by a weak keyword.

## Required audit structure

1. Ko tu patiesībā pārdod
2. Primārais buyer intent
3. Sekundārie buyer intent
4. Ko signalizē sludinājums — hero and gallery separately
5. Signal alignment score 1–10
6. Kas strādā labi
7. Konflikti un neskaidrības
8. EverBee tagu analīze
9. Vecums, favorīti un pieprasījuma signāli
10. Cena un piegāde
11. Trīs svarīgākās izmaiņas
12. Trūkstošās fotogrāfijas un infografikas, with the function of each
13. Hero idejas, kuras izdalīt no esošā sludinājuma
14. Hero idejas pieprasījuma pārbaudei

Finish by asking whether to prepare a two-column hero keyword list for EverBee.
