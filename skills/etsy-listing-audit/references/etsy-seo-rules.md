# Etsy SEO and Listing Rules

## Title

- Identify the primary product phrase and buyer intent; do not reward repetition merely because it uses more characters.
- Flag when the title mixes incompatible product identities, audiences or occasions.
- Flag quantity conflicts such as plural title language for a one-item offer.
- Do not rewrite the title unless requested.

## Category and attributes

- Check whether the Etsy category matches the product's actual function.
- A category mismatch can create irrelevant attributes and discovery signals.
- Treat attributes, materials and personalization as part of the offer, not only SEO fields.

## Materials and claims

- Materials should name actual materials, not benefits or keyword variants.
- Prefer an exact composition such as `100% European linen` over lists such as `natural fabric, durable textile, breathable material`.
- Claims such as organic, therapeutic, hypoallergenic, sustainable or certified require visible support. Recommend removing or substantiating them.
- Correct obvious certification and material-name inconsistencies.

## EverBee tags

When available, capture all 13 tags and show:

- tag phrase;
- Volume;
- Competition;
- Keyword Score;
- semantic relevance and decision.

Label the numbers as EverBee estimates. Do not treat a score of zero as automatic deletion: a precise low-volume phrase can still describe the offer well. Prioritize phrases that combine meaningful demand with exact product relevance.

Typical classifications:

- **Strong:** exact product/intent, useful demand and manageable competition.
- **Supporting:** relevant but broader or more competitive.
- **Weak:** generic decor, generic gift or unrelated audience term.
- **Risky:** unsupported claim or product type that conflicts with the offer.
- **Test separately:** plausible new hero direction that needs demand validation.

## Description

- Check whether the first visible lines clarify product, quantity, material, major benefit and ordering action.
- Identify conflicts with variations, hero images or infographics.
- Emoji-heavy or repetitive copy is not automatically wrong; flag it only when it reduces clarity or pushes critical information down.

## Conversion over keyword coverage

Do not recommend adding every plausible keyword to one listing. A focused listing should make one primary promise. Strong secondary intents can become separate hero listings when the product, photos and production support them.
